HOW TO PLAY

Your car accelerates automatically. Pass slower traffic and survive as long as you can.

Desktop: use Left / Right or A / D to steer, Space for nitro, S or Down to brake, and P or Escape to pause.

Mobile: drag anywhere on the road to steer with one finger, or use the on-screen buttons. Hold the lightning button for nitro and S to brake.

Traffic signals before changing lanes. Nitro drains while boosting and recharges when released. Armor adds extra health.

Earn 4 credits per clean overtake, a 10-credit close-call bonus and 25 credits per kilometer. Distance milestones award 180 credits.

Buy upgrades and skins between runs. Your progress is stored on this browser. Refreshing ends the current run and keeps checkpointed earnings.

Optional ads award 30 credits once per completed run. Skins use earned game credits; there are no real-money purchases.
